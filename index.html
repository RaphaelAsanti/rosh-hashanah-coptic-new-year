The Note

This year, Coptic New Year (1 Thout) and Rosh Hashanah fall on the same civil date.

In the Coptic tradition, Nayrouz marks the beginning of the liturgical year and carries the theme of resurrection — renewal, rebirth, and the victory of life over death.

In the Jewish tradition, Rosh Hashanah marks the beginning of the year and carries the theme of judgment — the opening of the books, the accounting of deeds, and the establishment of moral order.

This personal note simply records that resurrection symbolism and judgment symbolism coincide this year. No prediction or doctrinal claim is made. The observation is included because it aligns with the thematic structure explored in the ebook, where renewal and judgment are paired motifs.
